01

What grainsexuell is

An audio effect that chops what you play into loops, grains, slices and echoes, then layers and smears them into new sounds.

  • Four engines — Mosaic, Cloud, Shard, Tap — with four variants each: 16 characters.
  • An optional second engine (B), in series or in parallel.
  • Pitch wobble, low-pass filter and reverb after the engines.
  • Hold: freezes the last beat and keeps feeding it in.
  • A 60-second phrase looper with overdub, undo, half/double speed, reverse, quantize and burst.
  • Auto Gain, input/output gain, presets and MIDI learn.

Mono or stereo, any sample rate from 44.1 to 192 kHz. VST3 plugin and standalone app.

02

Getting started

In a DAW

  1. Restart the DAW so it rescans its plugins.
  2. Insert grainsexuell on an audio track:
    Logic Pro / GarageBand: Audio FX › MIDI-controlled Effects › beatsexuell › grainsexuell (it accepts MIDI for CC control, so Logic lists it there).
    Ableton Live, Bitwig, Reaper, Cubase and other VST3 hosts: Plug-ins › VST3 › beatsexuell › grainsexuell.
  3. Play into it. Guitar, keys, voice, drums, loops — short clear notes show it best.

Standalone

  1. Open grainsexuell from Applications.
  2. Options: pick your audio device, input and output. The first time, allow microphone access when macOS asks.
  3. Use headphones with a microphone to avoid feedback.

First five minutes

  1. Choose Init from the preset list — everything back to default.
  2. Try each engine and its variants A–D while you play.
  3. Sweep Swarm and Tail, the two biggest controls.
  4. Raise Haze for space, lower Dusk to darken.
  5. Browse the factory presets.

The window at a glance

LPink panel

Engine A (engine + variant), Engine B (Off or an engine, variant, Serial/Parallel) and Grain activity: events per second — loop grabs, grains, slices, echo repeats — with the last seconds as bars. Flat bars mean nothing is triggering.

TPreset

Click the name for the list, Save and Save as.

RRight column

Rows 01–11 (In … Out), Haze mode, Auto Gain and Hold switches, looper.

Handling

  • Drag a slider to change it; double-click to reset.
  • Switches show a square: on   off.
  • Right-click any control for MIDI learn.
  • Drag the bottom-right corner to resize (0.8×–2×). The size is remembered.
04

Signal flow

  1. Input
  2. In gain
  3. Auto Gain level in
  4. Hold adds the frozen beat
  5. Looper if Pre-FX
  6. Engine A → Engine B Serial, or A + B Parallel · Mix = dry/wet
  7. Bend pitch wobble
  8. Haze reverb
  9. Dusk low-pass
  10. Auto Gain level out + ceiling
  11. Looper if Post-FX · default
  12. Out gain
  13. Output
  • In gain hits everything, so it works like a drive.
  • Hold feeds the engines — frozen audio still gets chopped.
  • Bend, Haze and Dusk come after Mix, so they color the dry signal too.
  • Post-FX looper records what you hear (before Out). Pre-FX records your dry playing and the engines keep processing it, so the loop evolves.
05

The engines

Swarm, Tail, Span and Scatter are shared — but each engine reads them its own way.

AStack

All four layers: 0.5× 1× 2× 4×.

BLow

0.5× + 1×. Warm, an octave down.

CHigh

1× + 2× + 4×. Bright, octaves up.

DTumble

0.5× and 2× backwards.

Layered micro-loops. Keeps the last two seconds, grabs a short piece as a loop and plays it at several speeds at once — octaves down and up.

02Swarm

How often a new loop is grabbed, and how much of the high layers fades in.~3 s … ~0.1 s

03Tail

How long each loop lingers.

04Span

Loop length.25 – 750 ms

05Scatter

Random start and timing, wider stereo.

AMist

Soft, even texture.

BShimmer

Brighter, sparkling.

CMurk

Darker, thicker.

DBackwash

Grains running backwards.

Granular. Sprays up to 64 tiny overlapping grains from the recent past.

02Swarm

Grain density.~3 – 80 grains/s

03Tail

Feedback — grains of grains, a longer cloud.

04Span

Grain length.10 – 400 ms

05Scatter

How far back grains are picked, how loosely timed.

AShuffle

Random recent slices; Scatter adds reverse (up to 50%).

BCrush

Newest slice repeated, 12 → 3 bits, sample crunch.

CArp

Your last four notes as an arpeggio, steps up to an octave.

DStutter

A frozen slice up to 8×, each a little quieter.

Glitch, tempo-synced. Cuts the input into slices on the beat grid and rearranges them.

02Swarm

Slice size.1/4 … 1/32T

03Tail

Feedback — slices of slices.

04Span

How many recent slices Shuffle picks from.

05Scatter

Randomness (and reverse chance in Shuffle).

AEcho

Straight multitap delay.

BPing-pong

Echoes alternate left and right.

CReverse

Echoes play backwards.

DCascade

Uneven spacing, later echoes darker.

Multitap delay, tempo-synced. Four echoes on the beat grid, each quieter than the last.

02Swarm

Echo step, incl. triplets.1/1 … 1/16

03Tail

Feedback — high values ring for a long time.

04Span

Distance between taps.1 – 4 steps

05Scatter

Timing looseness.

NoteLike any feedback delay, Tap can get loud with high Tail on a held note. Turn on Auto Gain for a safety ceiling.
06

Engine B — serial and parallel

—Off

Only engine A runs. Default.

A→BSerial

B processes A's output. Tap into Cloud: echoes that dissolve into grains.

A+BParallel

Both process the input, mixed 50/50. Mosaic octaves beside Shard glitch.

10Mix B

B's own dry/wet. In Serial, 0 = only A.

B shares Swarm, Tail, Span and Scatter with A. Switching B or its routing fades smoothly, so you can change it while playing. B costs extra CPU. Factory presets turn B off; your saved presets remember it.

Sound controls

01In

Input gain. Down for hot signals, up to drive the engines. Not changed by presets.−24 … +24 dB · 0

02Swarm

How busy the engine is — density, rate, slice size.50%

03Tail

Repeats and feedback: how long things keep going.50%

04Span

Length: loop, grain, slice range or tap spacing.50%

05Scatter

Randomness in time, position and stereo. 0 = tight.0%

06Bend

Pitch wobble — vibrato, tape warble.0% = off

07Dusk

Low-pass filter. Fully up = off. Lower to darken and tame high layers.20 Hz … 20 kHz

08Haze

Reverb amount; type set by Haze mode.0% = off

09Mix

Dry/wet of the effect. 100% = effect only.50%

10Mix B

Dry/wet of engine B, when B is on.50%

11Out

Output gain, applied last. Not changed by presets.−24 … +24 dB · 0

Double-click any row to reset it.

09

Auto Gain

Off by default. When on, it levels the input so the engines see a steady signal, brings the output back to roughly the input level, and adds a soft ceiling so nothing goes over 0 dBFS.

Use it when a preset jumps in volume or high Tail gets too loud. With Auto Gain on, In acts as drive: the engines work harder, the output stays similar.

10

Hold

Freezes the last beat you played and loops it seamlessly into the effect.

One beat at the current tempo, between 0.15 and 2 s. Keep playing over it — both go through the engine. Hold on: the frozen beat fades in. Hold off: it fades out and recording resumes.

Good for a drone to play over, a chord frozen into Cloud, or something steady for Shard Stutter. Map it to a footswitch with MIDI learn.

11

The looper

Stereo, up to 60 s.

OffOff

Looper silent.

RecRecord

Records from the start. Leaving Rec sets the loop length.

▶Play

Plays the loop.

DubOverdub

Plays and adds what you play on top.

Options

½ 1 2Speed

Octave down / normal / octave up.

Reverse

Plays the loop backwards.

Quantize

Rounds the length to whole beats so the loop stays in time.

Burst

While on, re-triggers a short stutter every 1/16; the loop keeps running underneath.

↺Undo

First press removes the overdubs, the next clears the loop.

Pre PostPosition

Post-FX (default) records the finished sound. Pre-FX records your dry playing and sends the loop through the engines.

A basic loop

  1. Set Rec and play your phrase.
  2. At the end of the phrase, switch to Play — or Dub to keep adding.
  3. Undo if an overdub went wrong.
  4. Off to stop.
NoteLoop audio is not saved with your project or presets — record the output to keep it. Each instance reserves about 50 MB for the looper at 48 kHz, more at higher rates.

What a preset stores

Stored: engine, variant, Swarm, Tail, Span, Scatter, Bend, Dusk, Haze, Haze mode, Mix — user presets also store engine B.
Not stored: In/Out gain, Auto Gain, Hold and the looper. Switching presets never stops your loop or changes your levels.

Saving and sharing

Save writes an .xml file to ~/Library/grainsexuell/Presets (Finder › Go › Go to Folder…, paste, Return). The same files work on Mac and Windows.

The download has 40 more presets in its Presets folder. Drag the .xml files into the folder above — they appear under User. If Finder says the folder does not exist, save any preset once in grainsexuell first. Your DAW project saves the full plugin state, including window size and MIDI mappings.

13

MIDI control

  1. Route your controller's MIDI to the plugin. Some DAWs need MIDI input enabled for the effect.
  2. Right-click a control › MIDI learn.
  3. Move a knob, fader or button on the controller. Done.
  4. To remove: right-click › clear.

Every control can be mapped; mappings save with the project. Try Hold and looper mode on footswitches, Swarm and Tail on knobs, Mix on an expression pedal.

14

Tempo

Shard, Tap, Hold and looper Quantize/Burst follow the project tempo in a DAW, and a fixed 120 BPM in the standalone. Mosaic and Cloud are not tied to tempo.

16

Troubleshooting

The DAW doesn't list grainsexuell
Restart the DAW. Check that /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/grainsexuell.vst3 and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/grainsexuell.component exist. In Logic, look under MIDI-controlled Effects, not the normal effects list; if it is still missing, open Logic › Settings › Plug-in Manager and click Reset & Rescan Selection.
"Cannot be opened" / "unidentified developer"
The beta is not notarized by Apple yet. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to the grainsexuell line, then confirm with your password. On macOS 13 or older: right-click the package › Open › Open.
No input in the standalone
System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone: turn grainsexuell on.
No sound in the standalone
Options: check the device, that input channels are enabled, and the output is what you listen on.
Too loud or clipping
Turn on Auto Gain, lower Tail, or lower Out.
Clicks or crackles
Raise the audio buffer size. Engine B adds CPU. A click in the same spot every time is worth reporting with the settings.
MIDI learn does nothing
Make sure MIDI reaches the plugin (section 13).
Nothing happens when I play
Mix at 0? Dusk fully down? Check Grain activity and the In level.
A preset sounds wrong
Presets turn B off and leave In/Out, Auto Gain, Hold and the looper as they were — check those.
